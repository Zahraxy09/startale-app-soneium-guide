## What Is Startale?

**Startale Group** is a Web3 infrastructure and solutions company headquartered in Singapore, founded by **Sota Watanabe**. It is best known as the technical co-builder of **Soneium** — Sony's Ethereum Layer-2 blockchain — through a joint venture called **Sony Block Solutions Labs**.

Startale describes its mission simply:

> *"Build the next civilization by bringing the world onchain."*

The company operates across three pillars:

```
INFRASTRUCTURE:
  Soneium (Ethereum L2, built with Sony)
  Astar Network (Polkadot/Ethereum ecosystem)
  Strium (Layer 1 for tokenized securities, with SBI Holdings)

CONSUMER APPLICATIONS:
  Startale App (Super App / gateway to Soneium)
  Mini Apps ecosystem

FINANCIAL TOOLS:
  USDSC (Startale USD stablecoin)
  JPYSC (yen stablecoin, with SBI Holdings)
  RWA and tokenized securities infrastructure
```

Startale Group is not a typical crypto startup — it has institutional backing from **Sony**, **SBI Holdings** (Japan's largest financial conglomerate), and **Samsung Next**, which gives it unusual credibility and distribution access in the Japanese and global markets.

## Soneium — Sony's Ethereum Layer 2

**Soneium** is an Ethereum Layer-2 blockchain developed by Sony Block Solutions Labs — the joint venture between Sony Group Corporation and Startale Group.

### Key Facts

<cite index="63-1">Sony, best known for PlayStation, music, and film, has been backing and co-developing a public blockchain through Sony Block Solutions Labs since Soneium's mainnet launch in January 2025. Soneium is an Ethereum L2 that has facilitated more than 500 million transactions to date, all while boasting 5.4 million active wallets and upwards of 250 live applications.</cite>

```
Launch:              January 2025 (mainnet)
Testnet phase:       ~4 months, 14M+ wallets, 47M+ transactions
Transactions to date: 500M+
Active wallets:      5.4 million+
Live applications:   250+
Technology:          Optimism OP Stack (Superchain)
```

### Why OP Stack?

<cite index="60-1">Soneium uses Optimism's OP Stack technology and is integrated into the Optimism Superchain ecosystem, the same technical infrastructure powering Coinbase's Base network and Kraken's Ink network.</cite>

This means Soneium is part of the same technical family as Base (Coinbase's chain) — inheriting battle-tested infrastructure, standard Ethereum developer tooling, and interoperability with the broader Superchain ecosystem.

### Sony's Vision for Soneium

<cite index="63-1">Sony and Startale have framed Soneium as a way to bring Web3 to Sony's broad entertainment, gaming, and technology audiences.</cite> The applications now live on the network are clustered around fan engagement, digital collectibles, IP licensing, and AI-assisted experiences.

The goal, as Sota Watanabe put it, is to make Soneium the main onchain hub for entertainment on Ethereum — targeting Sony's existing audience of hundreds of millions of PlayStation, music, and film consumers who are not yet crypto-savvy.

## The Startale App — Super App for Web3

The **Startale App** (app.startale.com) is the all-in-one gateway to the Soneium ecosystem. <cite index="65-1">The Startale App combines the functionality of a crypto wallet, rewards center, and a platform for participating in airdrops and token generation events (TGEs). Users will be able to receive bonuses, exclusive tokens, and in-game assets related to projects in the Soneium ecosystem.</cite>

```
MISSIONS AND REWARDS:

MINI APPS:
  17+ mini programs embedded directly in the app

TGE AND AIRDROP ACCESS:
  Select projects distribute tokens through the Startale App
  STAR Points used as allocation mechanism
  Priority access to new token launches
```

### The Super App Vision

<cite index="65-1">Sota Watanabe said: "Our goal is to remove the last barriers preventing mass adoption of Web3. We've made working with blockchain as simple as using a regular app."</cite>

Experts have described the Startale App as a potential "App Store for Web3" — a single destination that hides all blockchain complexity while delivering the benefits.

## Account Abstraction — No Seed Phrases

The most important technical feature of the Startale App from a user experience perspective is **Account Abstraction (AA)**.

### What It Means

<cite index="75-1">The wallet you create is a smart account built on Startale's AA stack, which implements ERC-4337 standards and introduces features such as sponsored gas, smoother signing flows, social logins, and safer recovery options.</cite>

```
Traditional Web3 wallet:
  → Generate seed phrase (12-24 words)
  → Store it securely forever
  → If lost: funds gone permanently
  → Every transaction: manually pay gas in ETH
  → Signing flow: complex, technical

Startale App (AA wallet):
  → Sign up with email/social login
  → No seed phrase to manage
  → Gas fees: sponsored (free for users in many cases)
  → Recovery: social recovery options
  → Signing: simplified, app-like experience
```

This is why Soneium's testnet drew 14 million wallets — the barrier to entry dropped dramatically compared to standard wallets like MetaMask.

## USDSC — Startale's Dollar Stablecoin

**Startale USD (USDSC)** is the official dollar-pegged stablecoin of the Soneium ecosystem, launched December 3, 2025, in partnership with **M0**.

### What It Is

<cite index="59-1">USDSC was designed for payments and rewards within the Soneium ecosystem, as Japan embraces stablecoins as a payment alternative.</cite>

<cite index="60-1">The stablecoin is backed by short-term U.S. Treasuries and built on M0's modular stablecoin platform, which enables application-specific customization while maintaining shared liquidity across deployments.</cite>

```
Name:        Startale USD (USDSC)
Peg:         1:1 to US Dollar
Backing:     Short-term US Treasuries
Platform:    M0's universal stablecoin infrastructure
Network:     Soneium (Ethereum L2)
Purpose:     Payments, rewards, DeFi settlement, in-app transactions
```

### Why USDSC Matters

<cite index="61-1">USDSC is designed to act as the default stablecoin for payments, rewards, and transactions across the Soneium ecosystem. The network aims to bridge Sony's entertainment and consumer electronics businesses with Web3 applications, targeting mainstream adoption through familiar user experiences.</cite>

For Sony, having a native stablecoin means:
- In-game purchases without traditional payment processors
- Creator royalties paid instantly on-chain
- Fan rewards denominated in a stable asset
- Settlement infrastructure for tokenized content rights

### USDSC Earn Vault

Users can deposit USDSC into an earn vault within the Startale App to generate yield on their stablecoin holdings — turning idle stablecoins into productive capital while remaining within the Soneium ecosystem.

## STAR Points — The Rewards System

**STAR Points** is Startale's native incentive system that turns every onchain action into tracked, redeemable value.

### How to Earn STAR Points

| Activity | Points |
|---|---|
| Mint / purchase USDSC | ✅ Points awarded |
| Hold USDSC | ✅ Ongoing accumulation |
| Deposit USDSC in Earn Vault | ✅ Points + yield |
| Provide liquidity on Uniswap | ✅ Points |
| Complete missions | ✅ Points per mission |
| Interact with Mini Apps | ✅ Points per app |
| Daily check-in ("GM") | ✅ Daily streak bonus |
| Refer friends | ✅ Referral bonus |

### What STAR Points Unlock

<cite index="71-1">As Startale App develops, STAR Points will unlock additional benefits, including gas credits, ambassador-tier point multipliers, and access to priority token launches.</cite>

<cite index="61-1">Points may be redeemed for ecosystem programs, token launches, or community rewards. Select projects building on Soneium will distribute tokens and rewards through the Startale App, using STAR Points as the distribution mechanism for ecosystem benefits.</cite>

```
Current uses:
  - Access to project token launches
  - Gas credits (free transactions)
  - Priority access to new TGEs
  - Ecosystem program participation

Expected future uses:
  - Token airdrop allocation (official TGE not yet announced)
  - Ambassador tier multipliers
  - Governance participation
```

### The Carnival Campaign

<cite index="70-1">The Carnival features 17 Mini Apps with one quest each. Double rewards on every completion: STAR Points and Mini App Points. The first 1,125 completions on each of 8 participating Mini Apps earn $1 in $SUPER, on top of STAR Points and Mini App Points.</cite>

This "double rewards" mechanic — earning both Startale's STAR Points AND the individual Mini App's own token rewards — makes Startale App a powerful airdrop farming platform.

## Mini Apps — The Ecosystem Inside the App

Mini Apps are embedded programs built directly within the Startale App, allowing users to interact with Soneium ecosystem projects without leaving the app or managing separate wallets.

```
Mini App Model:
  Developer builds a Mini App → Listed in Startale App
  User opens Mini App → Completes a quest or action
  Rewards: STAR Points (from Startale) + Mini App's own points/tokens

Current categories of Mini Apps:
  - DeFi (swap, liquidity, lending)
  - Gaming and entertainment
  - Fan engagement and collectibles
  - AI-assisted applications
  - Creator tools and IP management
```

Startale Group launched **Startale Superstars**, a Mini App incubation program that helps projects build and launch within the Soneium ecosystem via the Startale App. This creates a flywheel: more Mini Apps → more user activity → more STAR Points → more ecosystem engagement → more Mini Apps attracted.

## Strium — The Next Layer

In February 2026, Startale and SBI Holdings unveiled **Strium** — a Layer 1 blockchain specifically designed for tokenized securities and Real World Asset (RWA) trading.

```
Strium:
  Type:    Layer 1 blockchain
  Focus:   Tokenized securities, RWA trading
  Partner: SBI Holdings
  Target:  The ~$19 trillion tokenization market (projected by 2033)
  
Why separate from Soneium?
  Securities require specific compliance controls
  Regulatory requirements differ from general DeFi
  Institutional-grade settlement finality requirements
```

This positions Startale as a multi-chain organization — Soneium for consumer Web3 entertainment, Strium for institutional RWA finance — with the Startale App serving as the user-facing gateway across both.

## Astar Network — Startale's Other Chain

**Astar Network** is Startale's earlier blockchain project — a multi-chain smart contract platform that supports both Polkadot and Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM) environments.

<cite index="77-1">Startale Group also operates Astar Network.</cite>

```
Astar Network:
  Technology:  Polkadot parachain + EVM compatible
  Status:      Live mainnet
  ASTR token:  Native token, publicly traded
  
Soneium relationship:
  Startale reinvests Soneium sequencer revenue into ASTR token purchases
  (Announced April 2025)
  Creates alignment between Soneium's success and ASTR token value
```

This is an unusual and interesting mechanism — profits from running Soneium's sequencer (the component that orders transactions on the L2) are used to buy back and support the ASTR token, creating a direct economic link between Soneium's adoption and Astar Network's tokenomics.

## Risks and Considerations

### STAR Points Are Not Tokens (Yet)

<cite index="72-1">STAR Points are Startale App in-app points that are not, and cannot be redeemed or exchanged with Startale for money or money's worth.</cite>

No official token launch (TGE) has been announced. STAR Points may influence a future airdrop, but this is not guaranteed or confirmed by Startale.

### Smart Contract Risk

The Earn Vault (for USDSC yield) involves smart contracts. Even institutional-grade infrastructure carries smart contract risk. Only deposit amounts you can afford to risk.

### Regulatory Risk

Startale operates in Japan — a jurisdiction with actively evolving stablecoin and digital asset regulation. USDSC's design reflects this (US Treasury backing, M0's compliance framework), but regulatory changes could affect operations.

### Sybil Detection

<cite index="72-1">Startale reserves the right to disqualify a user's reward eligibility if the Startale App Account is involved in any dishonest behavior (e.g., wash trading, illegally bulk account registrations/logins, self-dealing, sybil attacks, exploiting system loopholes, bot-driven or market manipulation).</cite>

Use one account per person. Sybil farming risks permanent disqualification.

### Closed Beta Limitations

The app remains invite-only / wave-based. Access is not guaranteed and may be limited in certain regions.
